# Welcome to Squadz!

Our little corner of the internet. A few things to get you started:

## House rules
- Be kind, we're all friends here.
- No spamming, and ask before inviting anyone new.
- Keep spoilers and loud stuff in the right room.

## Where to hang out
- **General**: day-to-day chat
- **Games**: game talk and planning sessions
- **Clips**: clips and the soundboard bot
- **Memes**: memes, obviously
- **Lounge** and **Game Night**: voice rooms

## Joining voice
Open **Lounge** or **Game Night** and tap the call / join button. Allow your microphone when asked.

## Soundboard
In **Clips**, type `!sb list` to see the sounds, `!sb play <clip>` to play one while you're in voice, and `!sb stop` to stop it.

## Emoji
Want a custom emoji or sticker? Send the image to the host and say what you'd like it called.
